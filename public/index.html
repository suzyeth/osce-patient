Interview practice, with the marks shown

A practice tool with a fictional person to interview. Cases are not reviewed by an expert. It does not give advice of any kind. If you need help with your own health, contact NHS 111 or your GP; practice stops if you start describing your own symptoms.

See what you get in 30 seconds

A finished report from a real case: every mark opens the question that earned it.

Start a station

  1. Choose a station and enter the access code.
  2. Take a history by typing, or speak through your keyboard's microphone. The patient only tells you what you ask about.
  3. Finish, or let the 8 minutes run out, for a report on what you covered and what you missed.

Under the hood, on Nebius Token Factory: Nemotron 3 Super judges each question against the published mark scheme; a second Nemotron 3 Super call plays the patient and sees only the facts you have unlocked; a third checks whether you are describing your own symptoms.
